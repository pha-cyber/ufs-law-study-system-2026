## Error-log protocol

Add a row for each meaningful misconception or repeated error.

Look for recurring patterns such as:

- Recall is accurate but application is weak.
- Similar doctrines are being confused.
- Cases are remembered but their legal principles are not.
- Correct rule is known but statutory section is missing.
- Facts are identified but not linked to the legal test.

Recurring errors should change the next study plan.
